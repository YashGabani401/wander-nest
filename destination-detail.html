day-wise itinerary

Arrive at Bora Bora Airport, transfer to your luxury resort via private speedboat, and settle into your overwater bungalow featuring panoramic turquoise lagoon views and a welcome sunset dinner.

Embark on a guided boat excursion to vibrant coral gardens. Swim alongside gentle stingrays, reef sharks, and colorful marine life in crystal-clear crystal waters.

Spend your morning relaxing on white-sand beaches or enjoying a Polynesian spa session, followed by a romantic evening sunset catamaran cruise before your departure transfer.
